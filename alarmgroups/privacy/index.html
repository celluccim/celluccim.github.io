Privacy policy

AlarmBatch · Last updated November 9, 2026

AlarmBatch is made by Maggie Cellucci. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the AlarmBatch app for iPhone.

The short version

Your alarms stay on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only outside service the app talks to handles in-app purchases.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your batches, alarms, labels, notes and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Sounds you import or record are converted and stored only on your device. The microphone is used only while you record a sound, and the recording never leaves your iPhone.
  • Alarms are scheduled with Apple's AlarmKit, which may show them on your Lock Screen and paired Apple Watch.

In-app purchases

Payments for AlarmBatch Pro are handled by Apple. We never see your name, email or payment details.

To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for this app (not linked to your name or Apple Account details), your purchase history for AlarmBatch, and basic technical data such as your device's OS version, country and IP address. It's used only to provide and restore your purchase, not for advertising or tracking.

Sharing a batch

When you share a batch or alarm, the app makes a link that contains the alarm details after the # in the web address. That part is never sent to a server: it goes only where you send the link, for example in Messages. If the link is opened in a web browser, GitHub Pages serves a short page explaining how to open it in the app; like any website host, GitHub may log the visitor's IP address.

Children

AlarmBatch doesn't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Your choices

Deleting the app deletes everything it stored on your iPhone. To ask about or delete the purchase records RevenueCat holds for your app identifier, contact us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its date.
