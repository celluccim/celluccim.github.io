Help and support

Questions, ideas or something not working? Email celluccimargaret@gmail.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version. We usually reply within a few days.

My alarm didn't ring

  • Open Settings in AlarmBatch and check Alarm permission says Allowed. If not, tap Open system settings and turn Alarms on.
  • Check the alarm and its batch are both switched on, and the batch's end date hasn't passed.
  • If an alarm says "Not set yet", tap it to set it.

Restore AlarmBatch Pro

Open Settings and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with. Pro carries across your devices.

Cancel or manage Pro

Open Settings, then Manage Pro, or go to the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions.

Open a shared batch

Tap the link on an iPhone with AlarmBatch installed. If it opens in Safari, go back to Messages, tap and hold the link, and choose Open in AlarmBatch.
